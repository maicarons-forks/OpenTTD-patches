# Rainy Weather

px-patch adds rainy weather to OpenTTD:


<DocFigure src="/images/rainy.png" alt="rainy" width="80" />


After turning on the `Random rain` option in the settings, the weather is rolled once per in-game month. In clear weather there is a 20% chance of rain, and in rainy weather there is a 50% chance of clearing up.


<DocFigure src="/images/rainy-setting.png" alt="rainy setting" width="80" />


The weather state (`Automatic`/`Always raining`/`Always clear`) can be adjusted in the `Sandbox options`


<DocFigure src="/images/rainy-sandbox.png" alt="rainy sandbox" width="80" />
