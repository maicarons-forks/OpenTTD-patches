# Cross-Company Train Coupling

Enabling cross-company train coupling requires turning on the "Allow coupling with trains owned by other companies" option in Shared Infrastructure:


<DocFigure src="/images/couple-share.png" alt="couple share" width="80" />


In the train information window, a company color indicator bar is shown on the left of every vehicle, indicating which company it belongs to:


<DocFigure src="/images/car-company-bar.png" alt="car company bar" width="80" />


The revenue and maintenance of a cross-company coupled train, as well as its ownership, all belong to the company owning the train with the "Go to couple" order.
