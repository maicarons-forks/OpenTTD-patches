# Cross-Company Vehicle Transport

Turn on the `Shared vehicle transport` option in the settings and set a suitable `Vehicle transport fee`; the fee for cross-company vehicle transport will be paid through the shared infrastructure income from the company owning the carried vehicle to the company owning the carrier.
