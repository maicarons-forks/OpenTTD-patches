# Train Decoupling

::: tip Note
the train coupling and decoupling features originate from the [OpenTTD YPS patch](https://github.com/dylansan/OpenTTD-YPS/tree/Decouple); px-patch ports this patch to the current version and improves upon its features.
:::


OpenTTD px-patch implements train coupling and decoupling for OpenTTD. Players can couple or decouple two trains inside a station, adjusting consists, orders, and more.
Based on this feature, we can achieve:
1. Reversing by decoupling the locomotive in a station and re-coupling on the other side of the platform
2. Two trains coupling and running together
3. Trains executing different orders after decoupling
4. Swapping between electric and non-electric locomotives
5. ...
