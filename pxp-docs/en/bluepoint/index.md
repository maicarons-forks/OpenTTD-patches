# Blueprint and Undo

::: tip Note
the blueprint and undo features originate from the [OpenTTD Blueprint patch](https://github.com/age77/openttd-15.3-blueprint); px-patch is based on this patch, with feature improvements.
:::


## Blueprint

The blueprint feature allows you to quickly copy your infrastructure, such as stations, without tedious and repetitive construction


<DocFigure src="/images/bluepoint.png" alt="bluepoint" width="80" />


## Undo

In the undo panel, you can undo (remove) your most recent construction actions, and view the recent construction history


<DocFigure src="/images/undo.png" alt="undo" width="80" />


_Note: the construction of custom airports cannot be undone_
